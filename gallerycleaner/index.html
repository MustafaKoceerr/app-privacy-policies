Gallery Cleaner Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Gallery Cleaner is an Android app that helps you clean up your photo gallery: you swipe through your photos and videos to keep, delete or postpone them, and the app suggests similar shots and junk photos. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

  • Your photos never leave your device. The app has no internet access.
  • We do not collect, store on servers, sell or share any personal data.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Nothing is deleted without your confirmation.

1. Data the app accesses

To work, the app needs access to the photos and videos on your device. Android asks you for this permission; on Android 14 and later you can choose to allow access to selected photos only.

  • Photos and videos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED, and READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 12 and earlier): to show them to you, analyze photos and move the ones you choose to the trash.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to show the progress of the photo analysis while it runs in the background.

The app does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, accounts or any other personal information.

2. On-device processing

All analysis (finding similar shots and photos that are blurry, dark, screenshots or otherwise likely junk) runs entirely on your device, using machine-learning models that are bundled with the app. Video content is not analyzed; only a video's length is used to suggest very short, likely accidental recordings. No photo, video, thumbnail or analysis result is uploaded anywhere. The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send data off your device.

3. Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following data in its private storage on your device:

  • a list of your gallery items with the metadata Android provides (identifier, date taken, dimensions, file size, folder, media type and video length),
  • your decisions (keep, delete, later),
  • analysis results (numerical image features and groups of similar photos),
  • your settings, including theme and language.

This data is only used to provide the app's features. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes all of it.

4. Deleting photos

The app never deletes anything automatically. Photos and videos are only removed when you choose to, after an in-app confirmation and the Android system confirmation dialog. They are moved to the system trash, where they remain restorable for 30 days.

5. Backup

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may back up the app's suggestion settings (for example similarity sensitivity and junk categories) to your Google account, as it does for other apps. This is handled by Android and Google, not by us, and we cannot access it. Your photos, decisions and analysis results are not included in this backup.

6. Data sharing and third parties

We do not collect any data, so we do not share or sell any data with third parties. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking. It includes a crash-reporting library (Firebase Crashlytics), but because the app has no internet access, no crash reports or other data are sent from your device.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at kocerlabs@gmail.com.
