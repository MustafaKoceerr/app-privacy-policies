TilePDF Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

TilePDF is a PDF and ebook (EPUB) reader for Android. Your documents and books are opened, read, annotated and saved entirely on your device. Books can't connect to the internet and scripts inside them don't run.

In short

  • Your files, their contents, file names, annotations and anything you type stay on your device. They are never sent anywhere.
  • TilePDF sends crash reports, so problems can be fixed. You can turn them off in Settings → Privacy.
  • TilePDF sends anonymous usage statistics only if you allow them. It asks once, and you can change your answer in Settings → Privacy at any time.
  • TilePDF has no ads, no advertising ID and no accounts, and the developer never sells or shares data.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

When TilePDF crashes, or a feature such as text recognition fails, the app sends a crash report to Google Firebase Crashlytics. A report contains:

  • the technical details of the error (the stack trace) and the app version;
  • the device model, Android version, available memory and storage, and whether the app was in the foreground;
  • a random installation ID created by Firebase, which isn't linked to your name, account or other apps;
  • if you allowed usage statistics, the last few statistics events before the crash (see below), such as "the reader was opened".

Crash reports never contain your documents, their text, file names or paths. They are kept for 90 days and used only to find and fix bugs. Crash reports are on by default because the app can't be kept working without them; turn them off in Settings → Privacy, and reports not yet sent are deleted.

Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

Only if you allow them, TilePDF counts which screens and features are used, for example "a document was opened", "a highlight was added" or "the dark page theme was chosen". Events carry categories only, such as a range of page counts ("11–50 pages"), never a document's name, content or exact size. Google Analytics also records the device model, Android version, app version, country (derived from the IP address, which isn't stored) and a random installation ID.

Statistics are never used for advertising: advertising features and the advertising ID are turned off, and the data is kept for 2 months. If you don't allow statistics, or turn them off later, nothing is collected.

Text recognition (Google ML Kit)

Text recognition on scanned pages runs on your device with Google ML Kit's built-in model; your pages are never sent anywhere. ML Kit itself may send Google technical usage information, such as the device model, the app version and how long recognition took, to improve ML Kit. It never contains your pages or recognised text.

Digital signatures

Checking digital signatures happens on your device, against the certificates Android itself trusts. Nothing is looked up online.

Who processes the data

Crash reports, statistics and ML Kit's usage information are processed by Google LLC on the developer's behalf, under Google's terms for Firebase. Data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

What the app stores, and where

Everything below stays in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Documents you open. TilePDF reads them through Android's file picker or when another app shares them. Some files are copied into private storage so they can be reopened later.
  • Annotations. Highlights, drawings and notes are saved into the PDF file itself, as is recognised text. Until they are saved, a draft stays in the app's private database. Highlights, notes and bookmarks in EPUB books stay in the app's private database; the book file isn't changed.
  • Library. The list of documents you opened, reading positions, favorites and bookmarks.
  • Preferences. Reading settings, theme, language and your privacy choices.

You can delete all of it by uninstalling TilePDF, or with Settings → Apps → TilePDF → Clear storage. Removing a document from the library also deletes its private copy and unsaved annotations.

Read aloud and selected text

  • Read aloud hands the book's text, sentence by sentence, to the speech engine installed on your device (for example Google's or Samsung's), which turns it into sound. TilePDF asks the engine for an offline voice. Whether your engine uses the network is set in Android's text-to-speech settings.
  • When you select text and pick an app such as a translator or dictionary from the menu, TilePDF passes that text to the app you chose, and only then.

Android backup

If Android backup is on, Android may include TilePDF's preferences in your own device backup. Your documents, annotations and library are not included. This backup is managed by Android and your Google account, and the developer cannot access it.

Permissions

TilePDF asks for no runtime permissions. It uses the internet permission only to send crash reports and, if allowed, statistics. The other install-time permissions come from Android libraries that save annotations in the background.

Your rights

You can turn crash reports and statistics off at any time in the app. Because the data is tied only to a random installation ID, the developer can't tell which data is yours; uninstalling TilePDF or clearing its storage starts a new ID. For any request or question about your data, including deletion, contact the developer below.

Children

TilePDF is meant for people aged 13 and over. It isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect data from them. Crash reports are on by default, and usage statistics are sent only if you allow them (users under 18 should ask a parent or guardian first). If you believe a child under 13 has used TilePDF, contact the developer below; because the data is tied only to a random installation ID, uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes the link.
